EKMOOL — BRAND LOGO KIT
=======================

THE MARK
A circular seal (the language of GI certification stamps) split by a soil
line: two leaves above, one turmeric-gold taproot below, tapering like the
numeral 1 ("ek") and breaking through the seal — the root runs deeper than
the badge.

FILES
- ekmool-logo-primary.svg      Stacked lockup — packaging fronts, hero use
- ekmool-logo-horizontal.svg   Website header, invoices, email signatures
- ekmool-mark.svg              Icon only — favicon, app icon, social avatar
- ekmool-logo-reversed.svg     For dark green backgrounds
- PNG exports                  Use only where SVG is not supported
- ekmool-favicon-512.png       Source for favicon.ico / app icons

COLORS
- Heritage Green  #1C3A2D   (primary ink)
- Turmeric Gold   #D99A2B   (root / accent — never recolor the root)
- Cream           #F5EFE2   (ink on dark backgrounds)

TYPEFACE
Wordmark & tagline: Marcellus (free, SIL Open Font License — download from
Google Fonts for any new layouts). Logo files need no font installed:
all text is converted to vector paths.

USAGE RULES
1. SVG is the master format — scales to any size, from favicon to carton.
2. Clear space around the logo: at least the height of the letter E.
3. Minimum sizes: stacked 90px tall, horizontal 32px tall, mark 24px.
4. One-color use: all-green or all-cream versions are acceptable;
   never place the gold root on a gold background.
5. Print/packaging: give the printer the SVG (or export vector PDF via
   Inkscape). Approximate CMYK: Green C82 M45 Y70 K45, Gold C15 M40 Y95 K3
   — confirm with a physical proof before mass printing.
6. Trademark: when filing, register the wordmark AND this logo (device
   mark) in classes 29, 30, 35.
